# Decision Memo: Personal LLM Portfolio Setup

**Date:** September 20, 2026

## Decision

I decided to organize my portfolio AI workflow around a central AGENTS.md file, a one-line CLAUDE.md reference, reusable skills, slash commands, and a running prompt log.

## Why

I wanted a system that keeps my preferences and standards consistent across future AI sessions instead of having to explain the same instructions every time.

## Key Standards

- Writing should sound professional but natural.
- AI should not invent accomplishments or credentials.
- Important facts should be verified.
- My portfolio should remain easy for recruiters and employers to understand.
- Meaningful AI-assisted work should be documented in the prompt log.

## Outcome

This structure gives me a reusable foundation that I can continue building throughout my courses and future professional projects.
