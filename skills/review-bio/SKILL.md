# Review Bio Skill

## Purpose

Review my professional bio and improve it while keeping it accurate, concise, and in my natural voice.

## Instructions

When reviewing my bio:

1. Keep it between 150 and 200 words unless I specify otherwise.
2. Preserve my natural voice and avoid writing that sounds overly formal or AI-generated.
3. Emphasize relevant experience in finance, private equity, entrepreneurship, business, and Division I athletics.
4. Do not invent accomplishments, numbers, credentials, or experience.
5. Check grammar, clarity, flow, and repetition.
6. Make sure the bio would make sense to a recruiter or employer who does not already know me.
7. Suggest edits rather than changing factual details without confirmation.
8. Confirm that any required AI-use disclosure is included.

## Output

Provide:

- A revised bio
- A short explanation of the most important changes
- Any factual claims that I should verify before publishing
